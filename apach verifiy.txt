# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 27 September 2026**

This application is designed with privacy in mind.

We do not intentionally collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.
We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking pixels,
fingerprinting, or behavioral tracking services.

We do not monitor your browsing history, searches, visited websites,
messages, files, contacts, location, microphone, or camera unless a
specific feature requires permission and you explicitly grant it.

The application does not send unnecessary personal or device information
to external servers. Data required for a feature is processed only for
that feature and is not retained longer than necessary.

We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies except where
strictly necessary for basic application functionality.

Third-party websites or services opened through the application may have
their own privacy policies and tracking technologies. This Privacy Policy
does not control those third-party services.

No internet-connected application can guarantee that nobody can observe
network traffic. Internet service providers, operating systems, network
administrators, or third-party websites may still process information
under their own policies.
